Sidequests

For fun, I code in the open and write about it.

Here's some whimsical stuff I particularly enjoyed to make:

  • tamRgo, a cyberpet that persists and grows between R sessions
  • pixeltrix, an interactive pixel editor in an R graphics window
  • r.oguelike, a tilebased 'game' in the R console with procedural dungeons and enemy pathfinding
  • londonmapbot (retired), an auto-tooter/tweeter of random satellite images in London

Sometimes I even write things that might be useful, like:

  • aftables to generate publication-ready best-practice spreadsheets (now maintained by the ONS)
  • jot, a lightweight Python-powered note-taking CLI
  • quartostamp, an RStudio add-in to insert Quarto document elements
  • gex, a dependency-light hex-logo creator for R

Your kilometerage may vary. Collaboration is welcome.
